YOUR DATA STAYS HERE

Use your own permitted daily open and close series. Exact header date,open,close; 200-2,000 rows; 200,000 bytes maximum. Increasing ISO dates, prices 0.01-1,000,000. Plain decimals only.

Use the same units and corporate-action adjustment for open and close. We cannot validate those, missing sessions, dividends or data rights. No calendar repair or data cleaning is inferred. Runs with wealth outside 1e-12 to 1e12 units are rejected.

Read in page memory only. No upload, storage or local-data export. Reset clears the page's reference, not secure erasure.

READ BEFORE COMPARING

A model, not a market.

All settings share 160 warm-up bars. The first evaluated open is row 161. Every eligible bar targets 100% long when the prior-close fast SMA exceeds the slow SMA; equality means cash. Differences within one part in a trillion of the larger SMA are treated as equality. This includes an initial entry if the rule is already long.

A 5 bps charge is 0.05% of each buy or sell notional, not a calibrated trading-cost estimate. Fractional shares are allowed. There are no partial fills, spread, impact, taxes, dividends, interest or corporate actions.

Opening prices may be impossible to obtain in live trading. Repeatedly changing settings can fit noise. No optimizer, predictive validation or investment recommendation is provided.
